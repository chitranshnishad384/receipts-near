Cold notes to founders cost nothing, so the people worth reaching never answer.

Receipts holds the message fee until they reply. One message to a sample founder locked 30 testnet dmUSD in a NEAR escrow; her reply released it. A second message set a 2-minute deadline, nobody answered, and the fee went back to the sender. The checkout prices that fee from Base, Ethereum, Arbitrum, or Solana with a NEAR Intents 1Click dry quote, then the sender locks the testnet stand-in without paying gas. dmUSD is not USDC. The quote does not move mainnet funds.
